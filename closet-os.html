🔁 Replace the Lulu brown chino short first
Your best short (waterproof, sweat-proof) is beat up. Search Lululemon for the Commission Short 7" in tan or olive — same properties. Consider buying two to future-proof.
👖 Upgrade your denim game (1 → 4 pairs)
You only have one pair of jeans with a zipper issue. For your NZ/old wealth aesthetic, look for slim-straight fits in dark indigo and mid-wash. Rodd & Gunn makes excellent NZ-inspired denim. Also try APC or Corridor for quality thrift-able jeans.
👕 Find your best pocket tee twin
Your black Amazon pocket tee (possibly G Scissors) rated 10/10 — search Amazon for "cooling pocket tee 100% cotton" to identify and buy 3–4 more. This is your anchor piece.
🎽 Short-sleeve textured polos (0 owned) — biggest gap
Rodd & Gunn make exactly what you described — 2-button placket, relaxed collar, textured fabric. Their Sports Fit Polo is ideal for your aesthetic. Look for olive, navy, and stone colorways.
🪡 Chambray shirt fix
Look for a Japanese selvedge chambray or a Rodd & Gunn linen-blend with horn or corozo (wood-like) buttons. Aim for a slightly oversized relaxed fit for layering over tees. Dark chambray is more versatile than light for PNW weather.
🧥 Replace the Banana Republic rain jacket (4/10)
For Seattle rain + your outdoor/VC aesthetic: look at Arc'teryx Veilance (luxury), Helly Hansen (functional), or Rodd & Gunn's Queenstown jacket. Target a technical shell in olive, navy, or stone — not black, which is covered by the Lulu cargo.
🕶 Sunglasses
For your aesthetic: Moscot, Oliver Peoples, or Maui Jim (outdoor/NZ). Stick to tortoiseshell, olive, or matte black frames. Polarized essential for Pacific Northwest glare on water.
🌿 Fragrance — sandalwood + cypress
Tom Ford Oud Wood, Comme des Garçons Wonderwood, or Aesop Hwyl. For NZ/outdoor feel: Margiela Replica "Sailing Day" or Paco Rabanne Pure XS Forest. Try decants before committing to a full bottle.
🎨 Palette note — the red Vuori pants
Red doesn't slot naturally into your minimalist/old wealth palette and you've never worn them. Consider donating and freeing that slot for a navy or olive pair instead.
Thrift shop hunting list — sorted by priority
🔴 High priority
Slim-straight dark indigo jeans, 32×30/31 — Brands: APC, Nudie, Patagonia, Levi's 511. Pass if faded, worn knees, or wide leg.

Short-sleeve polo, textured fabric, M — 2-button placket, no logos, pique or ottoman texture. Colors: olive, navy, stone, slate. Pass if synthetic blend or baggy fit.

Lululemon Commission or ABC Short, brown/tan, 32W — Must be waterproof/technical fabric. Check gusset and zipper integrity.
🟡 Medium priority
Dark chambray or denim shirt, S/M — Look for horn or wood-look buttons (not plastic). Rodd & Gunn, Faherty, or any Japanese selvedge maker. Pass if plastic buttons or boxy fit.

Solid cotton tees, M, heavier-weight (180gsm+) — Slight wash/worn look. Colors: off-white, slate, olive, stone. Brands: Sunspel, vintage Hanes, or any quality thrift find.

Merino quarter-zip or mid-layer, S/M — Brands: Icebreaker, Smartwool, Rodd & Gunn. Colors: charcoal, navy, olive. Pass if pilled or synthetic.
🟢 Opportunistic
Technical rain shell, M, fully waterproof — Check for taped seams. Colors: olive, navy, stone. Brands: Arc'teryx, Marmot, Patagonia, Helly Hansen. Pass on Banana Republic or anything without taped seams.

Brown leather chukkas — Clarks Desert Boot or similar. Check sole wear and leather condition. Only buy if stitching is intact.

Canvas or twill athletic shorts, M 32 — Lululemon or Vuori preferred. Check inner liner for sweat-proof fabric.
